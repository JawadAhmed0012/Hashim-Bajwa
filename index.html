Leadership in Education and Quality Assurance

"My approach is grounded in integrity, collaboration, accountability and continuous improvement. I focus on strengthening quality, creating educational opportunities and delivering positive outcomes for learners and organisations."

As Managing Director of Bespoke Professionals Limited, I provide strategic leadership across education, vocational training, student recruitment, quality assurance and business development.

I am a British Council Certified UK Education Agent Counsellor, ICEF Certified Education Agent and Lead Internal Quality Assurer. My experience includes student recruitment, Information, Advice and Guidance, regulated qualifications, assessor development, standardisation, contract management and stakeholder engagement.

I have worked with awarding organisations, universities, colleges and employers, while also supporting T Level delivery for Newham College and coordinating industry placements through Unifrog.
